Retina
Neurosensory Tunic ★ PRIMARY DR TARGET

The light-sensitive neurosensory layer lining the posterior eye. Photoreceptor cells (rods and cones) convert light photons into neural action potentials transmitted via ganglion cell axons.

CLINICAL PERSPECTIVE: DIABETIC RETINOPATHY TARGET: Chronic elevated blood glucose destroys retinal capillary pericytes, causing microaneurysms, hemorrhages, hard lipid exudates, macular edema, and vision-threatening neovascularization.
